The benchmark.html fixture, its three automated browser recordings, measurement
logs, and authored edit recipes in this directory are dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These are controlled recordings of actual browser microbenchmarks. They contain
no third-party video, speech, people or product performance claims. Their added
diagnostic soundtrack is synthetic, not a human voice. They are not the builder's
hero clips. Machine timing varies; rerun scoring on the frozen MP4s rather than
re-recording them and treating a different source as the same evaluation.
