# SHOWME hero demo flow

**Status:** target demo outline; not yet validated end to end. Do not record this as a capability claim until each stage passes the experiment criteria.

```text
Expert demonstrates one safe, bounded task
  ↓
SHOWME proposes objects, actions, states, and evidence
  ↓
Human inspects the candidate process
  ↓
Second person performs the task
  ↓
SHOWME recognizes correct progress
  ↓
Learner makes a deliberate, recoverable mistake
  ↓
SHOWME detects the divergence and grounds its explanation
  ↓
Learner corrects it; SHOWME recognizes recovery
  ↓
Learner asks “what if?”; SHOWME visualizes a bounded procedural outcome
```

## Three-minute target

1. **0:00–0:15 — Reality first.** “Tutorials can show you what to do. They can’t see what you’re doing.” Show the task and learner, not an architecture slide.
2. **0:15–0:40 — Demonstration.** Show the expert clip and the process candidate with inspectable evidence.
3. **0:40–1:05 — Correct progress.** Show one or two states recognized in a separate learner attempt.
4. **1:05–1:45 — Hero moment: mistake.** Introduce the deliberate error. SHOWME identifies the observed mismatch, cites the frame, and points to the relevant object/location. The learner corrects it; SHOWME detects recovery.
5. **1:45–2:15 — Hero moment: alternative.** Ask one bounded “what if?” question and show its predicted procedural state and violated prerequisite. Label this as procedural simulation.
6. **2:15–2:45 — How it works.** Briefly reveal model-produced process/state proposals, visual grounding where validated, and deterministic evidence/schema checks.
7. **2:45–3:00 — Impact.** Close on preserving and transferring human know-how. State clearly which parts are prototype and what the system cannot yet do.

## Evidence and honesty rules

- Use separate learn, correct, error, and recovery clips; keep human labels out of model inputs.
- Preserve the raw model observations and show what local validation does separately.
- No timestamp replay, manually authored learner labels, or prompt leakage presented as learning.
- If any hero moment fails repeatably, cut or label it as a planned capability; do not hide the failure behind edits or UI.
- Include footage rights and consent in the submission checklist.
