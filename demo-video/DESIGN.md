# CONTEXT demo visual identity

## Style Prompt
A restrained investigative film built from the real CONTEXT workstation. Dark
graphite, warm paper text, copper for omissions, muted blue for supported matches.
Typography should separate human-readable claims from precise evidence. Preserve
actual application footage and leave its findings legible. No invented UI.

## Colors
- #111315: graphite canvas
- #e9e8e3: primary paper text
- #b8c2c6: supporting text
- #e6a06c: omission and narrative emphasis
- #93bada: supported correspondence and timestamp citations

## Typography
Space Grotesk for headlines and narration summaries; IBM Plex Mono for chapter
numbers, evidence figures and provenance. App captures retain their native fonts.

## Motion
Gentle 0.4-second crossfades between chapters. Brief entrances, no dramatic camera
moves. Actual screen interactions run at normal speed. Reveal footage is shown
in real time. Do not remove a network wait without labeling the cut or cache.

## What NOT to Do
- No fake findings or fabricated model answers.
- No neon, glass panels or excessive gradients.
- No generic dashboard stock imagery.
- No accuracy claim extrapolated from the small evaluation set.
- No implication that a detected omission proves deception.
