# Core source-screening notes for Antigravity

28 October 2026; official pages inspected. This is a source-level access check, not six selected/downloaded clips. Antigravity owns exact case selection in NATURAL_DATASET_SELECTION.md; these notes avoid duplicating that work.

- PEViD-UHD: https://www.epfl.ch/labs/mmspg/downloads/pevid-uhd/ . 26 sequences, 13 seconds each; UHD/HD/SD; signed research consent; person silhouette XML annotations. Download section uses FTP, not a ready direct per-clip HTTPS URL. Copyright permits research use/modification/distribution, excludes commercial distribution. Do not treat silhouettes as plate/screen corners. Public-demo and cloud-use suitability are not established merely by research consent. No files downloaded here.
- LSVTD: https://davar-lab.github.io/dataset/lsvtd.html . 129 clips, training/testing XML. Image and annotation terms CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Page has both email wording and an external open-access link; exact working per-clip access remains unverified. No email sent and no files downloaded.
- BDD100K: https://github.com/bdd100k/bdd100k . Toolkit README confirms 100,000 forty-second videos and vehicle/person tasks; that does not establish plate-corner annotations. Toolkit BSD license is not proof of dataset terms. Berkeley data site timed out during browsing. No exact clip or download permission verified here.
- CCPD: https://github.com/detectRecog/CCPD . Large annotated still-image collection with Google Drive/Baidu download links. Useful for separate geometry checks, not a continuous-video case. Do not animate stills and label them real temporal footage.
- CoC: https://zenodo.org/records/21790608 . Files restricted; non-commercial research/educational terms; redistribution needs author permission. Screen-recapture/deepfake dataset, not verified screen-geometry tracking labels. Not a ready sample download for today's execution.

The two self-recorded benchmark slots remain unavailable until actual files are supplied. Existing LEGO media is not a relevant screen/plate substitute. Existing generated development footage must stay outside independent-case counts. Do not invent six filenames to fill the manifest.
